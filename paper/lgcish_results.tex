\noindent All numbers are produced by the reproducible evaluation suite
(\texttt{python evaluation/generate\_all.py}) on a codebook of $N{=}40$
visually distinct $512{\times}512$ images (UCID\,/\,Kodak\,/\,USC-SIPI),
$\log_2 40\approx5.322$ bits/image. \textbf{Each table is followed immediately by
the figure that visualises it.}
